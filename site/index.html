The problem

Revenue is not the amount you keep.

An order can carry several marketplace costs at once. Offsite Ads and manual ad spend can make two similarly priced sales produce very different margins. This workbook keeps the calculation at order level so the difference stays visible.

What it subtracts

The fees sellers usually have to piece together.

Zero by default for US sellers and editable for other locations.

Questions

Before you download

Are the fee rates fixed?

No. Every fee assumption is editable in SETTINGS. The included US defaults are starting points and must be verified before use.

Does it calculate taxes or cost of goods?

No. It does not calculate income tax, VAT on seller fees, sales tax collected by Etsy, shipping labels, currency conversion, subscriptions, or cost of goods.
