## Description

Etsy Seller Profit Tracker (After Fees) is an editable Excel workbook for sellers who want to understand what remains from each order after marketplace costs. Add one order per row, including item price, shipping charged, quantity, Offsite Ads attribution, Etsy Ads spend, and any refund. The spreadsheet then calculates item subtotal, net sales after refund, listing fees, transaction fees, payment processing, Offsite Ads, regulatory operating fees, total fees, net profit, and margin.

All fee assumptions live in a dedicated SETTINGS sheet, with an official source and checked date beside each default. The starting values are marked for a US seller and include the 15% or 12% Offsite Ads switch. A monthly dashboard summarizes net sales, fees, profit, margin, order count, and attributed orders. Eight completed examples make the logic easy to audit before entering your own data.

You receive one `.xlsx` file for Microsoft Excel or another compatible spreadsheet app. This is an independent planning tool, not tax advice, accounting advice, or an Etsy product. Fees vary by country and date; always confirm them in your Etsy Payment account.
